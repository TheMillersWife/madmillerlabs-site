Architecture
01 / COMPUTE

Nutanix HCI

Nutanix CE 6.8.1 on AHV. Single-node cluster with full Prism Element management, CVM, and AHV host. Mirrors the production Nutanix environment at KKI.

02 / IDENTITY

Dual AD forests

Forest A (madmillerlabs.com) and Forest B (megacorplab.com) administered independently to simulate a pre-merger state. Tiered account model, gMSA, break-glass procedures.

03 / NETWORK

Segmented fabric

13 VLANs across management, identity, app, security, VPN, and red team segments. Explicit inter-VLAN ACLs on pfSense. DHCP relay, split-brain DNS, OpenVPN with DDNS.

04 / SECURITY

Defense-in-depth

Wazuh SIEM, Suricata IDS, Velociraptor EDR, Sysmon. PAW jumpbox with Credential Guard. Break-glass accounts with automated Wazuh alerts on use.
